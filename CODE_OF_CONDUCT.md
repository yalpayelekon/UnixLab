# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in UnixLab a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Focus on what is best for the community.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, and publishing
another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the
security contact described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Maintainers will
review reports promptly, protect the reporter's privacy, and apply
proportionate corrective action. Maintainers who do not follow this code may
be removed from the project team.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
